# Scribe privacy policy

- The Scribe program on your PC connects only to Scribe's own services: the Scribe hands hub it was paired with during setup, and, during setup, the Scribe Guard key endpoint to fetch a public key.
- It sends those services: a device id for this PC, the program's online status, and the results of work requested through your own Scribe account (for example command output or build results from the folders you allowed).
- It does not collect browsing history, keystrokes, files outside the allowed folders, or passwords for AI services.
- Your Scribe passphrase is used once during setup and is not stored.
- To stop all transfer: uninstall Scribe, or delete the `Scribe` scheduled task.

Scribe's address is scribe.dairyforge.com; AIs connect at https://scribe.dairyforge.com/mcp.
Contact: the repository owners via GitHub issues.
